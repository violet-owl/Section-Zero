24/05/26, 04:09 - +1 (313) 410-5315: _*TLDR:* Network early if it helps your goals, but do not become a feral shut-in . Have the coffee chats, learn interesting things, then go be a normal human._

In terms of how to handle networking, *I’ll be the slight contrarian here.* Definitely do not burn yourself out over the summer, but some recruiting paths, especially less structured ones like VC and healthcare, can benefit from early networking and preparation.

That does not mean you cannot recruit successfully without summer prep. It just means that, for people who already have a clear interest in a less formal pathway, starting early can be helpful. In my case, I came in genuinely excited about Healthcare VC, so speaking with people over the summer felt less like work and more like a chance to learn what interesting healthcare innovations are being built. I was not losing sleep over it, but I do think those early conversations ultimately helped my internship recruiting process.

My advice would be to use good judgment and hold yourself accountable to what you actually want. If early networking aligns with your interests, it can be valuable. If it feels forced or is taking away from your ability to recharge, then it may not be worth it. Part of the MBA process, both admissions and recruiting, selects for intelligent, level-headed people (mostly). So I *hope* everyone can interpret this advice responsibly and apply it when appropriate.
